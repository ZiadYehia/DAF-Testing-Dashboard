## Business Purpose

The landing screen is the entry point of the EPTTS Mobile app. It presents two paths:
- **Sign In** — for staff accounts (role-determined by credentials)
- **Patient** — public access for patients to verify pharmaceutical products

## Happy Path

1. Launch the EPTTS app.
2. The EDA-branded landing screen appears.
3. Two buttons are visible: **Sign In** (primary) and **Patient** (secondary).
4. User taps the relevant button to proceed.
